FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Email [email protected] — we usually reply within an hour.

Is there a free trial?
Yes — 14 days, full Growth plan, no credit card required. After that you can keep going on any plan or cancel with one click.
What does "per user" mean?
Each user is a named team member with login access. Customers using your portal don't count — only your internal team does. Field crew with mobile-only access counts too.
Can I switch from Build-your-own to All Apps later?
Yes, anytime. We'll prorate the difference. Most customers start with 3–5 apps, then move to Growth within 4 months to unlock the full platform.
Are there setup or migration fees?
No. Onboarding is included on every plan. We migrate contacts, deals, projects, invoices, and file attachments at no charge. Most workspaces are live within 7 days.
What's the difference between yearly and monthly?
Yearly billing saves 20% versus monthly. Both can be canceled anytime — annual cancellations are prorated and refunded for unused months.
Do you offer non-profit or contractor association discounts?
Yes — 25% off for verified non-profits, and we partner with trade associations (NAHB, PLANET, NKBA) for member-only pricing. Email us to verify.
What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, ACH (US), SEPA (EU), and invoice billing for annual Scale plans.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. One click from your account settings. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period; yearly plans are prorated and refunded. We'll export all your data on request.
